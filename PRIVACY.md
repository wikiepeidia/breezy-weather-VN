# Privacy Policy

This hobby fork does not collect personal data.

Optionally, it can access your approximate or precise location to find weather data for your position. That data is shared with the weather/location sources you choose to use. If you do not want to share your location, deny the permission and choose a city manually.

If you enable an app-update check in a build where that feature is configured, the app may connect to GitHub to compare the latest available release with the version installed on your device. The comparison happens on device. [GitHub Privacy Policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

The icon-pack feature requires listing installed packages so the app can detect compatible icon packs. Packages that do not match a supported icon-pack format are ignored. No package list is uploaded anywhere.

This fork relies on third-party APIs to obtain weather data, location search, reverse geocoding, or optional current-location lookup. Review the privacy policy of each source before enabling it, and only use the ones you agree with.

This fork can optionally share your location and weather data with other apps. Review the privacy policy of any receiving app before granting that permission.

This privacy policy may be updated if behavior changes or wording needs clarification. The latest version is always the one published in this repository.
